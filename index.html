Our Story

About NanoCab

NanoCab is one of the leading Wire & Cables manufacturing companies in India and an end-to-end solution provider in the Power Distribution Equipment industry which based in Delhi, dealing in electrical products in the Eastern, Northern & Western parts of India for the last 10 years.

It was founded by Mr. Rajesh Jain and Mr Adish Jain. They have excelled and taken this company to the succeeding level. Mr. Umang Jain & Ujjwal Jain, Sons of Rajesh and Adish Jain have taken the batons handed over from their fathers and are infusing young blood into the organization. All employees and dealers at NanoCab are regarded as the Pariwar Members.

Experience & Approvals

Our field of experience covers all types of electrical services in Projects, Retails, Showrooms, Health Centre, Real-Estate, Schools and Universities and many more. Our products are approved by all major Govt. & Semi Govt. Depts., Public and Private Companies in major parts of India. All our products are certified by B.I.S.
